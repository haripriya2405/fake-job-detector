# Business Discovery & Market Requirements

## 1. Problem Statement
Job search scams have risen over 118% year-over-year. Scammers impersonate top tech firms, post bogus remote data entry/intern roles, extract personal PII (SSN, ID scans, banking info), and demand upfront "equipment fees" or "training certification deposits".

## 2. Target Audience
- **University Students & Entry-Level Job Seekers**: High vulnerability to fake internships and remote work scams.
- **Career Changers & Remote Seekers**: Frequent targets of high-salary "too good to be true" data entry schemes.
- **University Career Centers & Bootcamps**: Require automated verification tools to protect alumni cohorts.
- **Talent Platforms & Recruiters**: Brand protection against impersonation.

## 3. Core Value Proposition
- Instant multi-modal analysis (Text, PDF offer letter, WhatsApp/Telegram screenshot).
- Transparent 0–100 risk score breakdown with evidence highlight snippets.
- Specific, actionable guidance (e.g. "Do not transfer money for equipment", "Verify recruiter email domain").
